This policy explains what information Wilson Window Cleaning Services ("Wilson Window Cleaning", "I", "me", "my") collects, why I collect it, and what I do with it. Wilson Window Cleaning Services is a sole proprietorship based in Hood River, Oregon.

Short version: I collect only what I need to quote and carry out window cleaning work, I use it only for that, and I do not sell it to anyone.

1. Information I collect

When you request a quote, book a job or contact me, I may collect:

  • Your name
  • Contact details — mobile or landline number and email address
  • Service address and details about the property that are needed to quote and do the work, such as the number and type of windows and access arrangements
  • Job and scheduling information — what you booked, when, and our messages about it
  • Payment status — whether an invoice is paid or outstanding. Card details are handled by the payment provider and I never see or store full card numbers.

This site itself does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. Booking is handled by an external booking provider linked from this site; once you follow that link, that provider's own privacy policy also applies.

2. How I use it

  • To prepare and send you a quote
  • To schedule, confirm and carry out the work
  • To let you know when I am on my way, or if weather forces a change
  • To invoice you and keep track of payment
  • To follow up after a job, and to remind you when a recurring clean is due
  • To keep basic business records as required by law

I do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling, and I do not sell it.

3. Mobile information and text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

If you opt in to text messages, I use your mobile number only to send the service messages described in the text messaging terms — quotes, scheduling, confirmations, arrival notices, follow-ups and invoices.

Your opt-in consent is never sold, rented or shared for anyone else's marketing. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, and you can reply HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply.

Consenting to text messages is not a condition of booking or receiving service.

4. When information is shared

I do not sell, rent or trade your information. I share it only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers who run the tools I use — the booking and messaging platform, and the payment processor. They may only process your information to provide those services to me, and may not use it for their own marketing.
  • When the law requires it — for example in response to a valid legal request, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

5. How long I keep it

I keep customer and job records for as long as you are a customer and afterwards for as long as needed for business, tax and accounting purposes. Text messaging opt-in and opt-out records are kept as long as needed to show that consent was properly obtained and honored. You can ask me to delete your information at any time and I will do so unless I am required to keep it.

6. Your choices

  • Stop texts — reply STOP to any message, or ask me directly.
  • Change how I contact you — tell me if you would rather be reached by phone or email only.
  • See, correct or delete your information — email or call me and I will take care of it.

If you are an Oregon resident, you may have additional rights under the Oregon Consumer Privacy Act regarding access to, correction of and deletion of your personal data. Contact me using the details below and I will respond.

7. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information, and I keep it on password-protected devices and accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

My services are directed at adults. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided me with information, contact me and I will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
