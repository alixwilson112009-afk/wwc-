What I clean

Homes and small businesses across the Gorge — glass first, plus the jobs that go with it.

Residential windows

Inside and outside, however your windows are laid out. Most homes take a couple of hours and you don't need to be there for it.

Screens, tracks & sills

Screens come out and get washed, tracks and sills get wiped down. It's the part people notice most once it's done.

Upper-story & hard-to-reach

A water-fed pole reaches second-story glass from the ground, so there are no ladders leaning against your siding or gutters.

Commercial & storefront

Storefronts and small offices, on a regular schedule if you want one. Early starts available so I'm finished before you open.

Gutter cleaning

Gutters cleared out so the water actually goes where it should. Debris bagged and hauled off, downspouts checked before I leave.

Solar panels

Dust and pollen cut into what your panels produce. Same pure water I use on glass, no detergents and nothing abrasive on the surface.

Why people call me back
